# Final project proposal

Replace this file with a short proposal for your final project. Your project should use one or
more types of data from the course (tabular, text, or spatial data) and present the results as a
website, a report, or a dashboard.

Your proposal should include:

- A title and the question you will ask.
- The names of your team members.
- The data you will use, with a link to each source.
- Whether you will make a website, a report, or a dashboard.
